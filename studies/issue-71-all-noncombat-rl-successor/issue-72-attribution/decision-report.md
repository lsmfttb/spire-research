Each alternative has one terminal suffix sample. For a root state s and action a, the receipt yields one realized R_a, not the conditional distribution of R given s,a. The observed selected label max(R_a - R_a0, 0) is a one-draw clipped contrast; in general E[max(R_a-R_a0,0)] is not max(E[R_a|s,a]-E[R_a0|s,a],0). With one suffix per action, conditional variance Var(R|s,a), expected advantage, and its uncertainty are not identified. A common simulator seed does not guarantee common random numbers: different actions may consume different RNG streams/counters or shift subsequent draws. Exact capture/restore replay proves repeatability for the same checkpoint and action path, not cross-action RNG equivalence. Consequently branch labels are noisy signed outcomes, not action-value estimates or statistical significance.
