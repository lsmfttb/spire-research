## Confirmed gaps and limits

1. **Choice-set conditional context loss is exact.** Old `features()` includes `public_state`, one option, legal count and ordinal. The adapter put other choices in `legal_options`, not common state. Native-produced fixture holds discard target ordinal 7 and legal count 9 fixed: changing another visible card or offered potion gives old maximum feature difference **0**, while v2 differs. Argmax still compares separate candidate scores: this is fixed-target context omission, not total blindness to alternatives.
2. **Group association is public before treatment.** Native card candidates use group `idx1` and card `idx2`; no opaque bits are required. The source does not add a standalone group ID to card semantics. Old visible treatment drops the indices. v2 derives local group references before dropping executor identity.
3. **Native reward card `misc` and exact `upgrade_count` are absent.** Deck cards expose identity, upgraded boolean, exact upgrade count and misc. Reward cards expose identity/name/type/rarity and upgraded boolean only. Changing underlying reward misc leaves the native reward payload identical. A normalizer cannot recover absent native fields. Synthetic misc-bearing public fixtures test a proposed contract, not this native pin's observed availability. Whether particular offer types require those additional inspectable facts is a remaining visibility question; no mechanics fix is made here.
4. **Full inventory take forfeits the offer.** `obtainPotion` returns without acquisition at full capacity (also under Sozu); `executeRewardsAction(POTION)` then removes the offer. This is source-confirmed and executed in the native synthetic fixture. It is a strategic transition-fidelity caveat, not permission to insert local potion rules or retention heuristics.
5. **One card group is consumed per take.** The native fixture begins with two groups, discards a held potion, then picks from group 0. Both groups survive discard; the second group survives card take and becomes group index 0. The offered potion survives until its own take. Existing stale group indices must be resolved against fresh candidates, not retained across the transition.
